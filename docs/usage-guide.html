Attorney Workflow

The Attorney Experience

You stay in complete control. The platform removes the repetitive work of data entry and document assembly while ensuring every output matches your exact standards.

KEY BENEFITS
Save 60–80% of mechanical drafting time
Maintain 100% of your template language
Consistent, coordinated 8-document packages
Full audit trail of all client data and generations
01
Set up your firm & templates
Upload your existing Word templates (revocable trust, pour-over will, POAs, healthcare directive, etc.). The system never modifies your language — it only injects client data.
One-time setup per document type
02
Invite a client
Send a secure, branded invitation via email. The client receives a magic link and completes the intake at their own pace.
Magic link Auto-save progress Mobile friendly
03
Review client answers
All answers appear in your dashboard in a clean, organized view. You can see progress, flag issues, and add internal notes before generating documents.
04
Generate the full estate plan
With one click, generate a complete, coordinated package of 8 documents using your exact templates:
Revocable Living Trust
Pour-Over Will
Durable Financial POA
Advance Healthcare Directive
HIPAA Authorization
Certificate of Trust
Personal Property Memo
Trust Funding Instructions
All documents clearly marked DRAFT – For Attorney Review Only
05
Review, download, and deliver
Review the generated package in your dashboard. Download the full ZIP or individual documents. Make any final professional adjustments in Word (as you normally would), then deliver to the client.
For Your Clients

Client Experience

A guided, respectful process

Clients complete a modern, adaptive questionnaire instead of a confusing 20-page PDF. The experience feels modern and supportive while still being thorough.

WHAT CLIENTS APPRECIATE
  • Save progress and return anytime
  • Clear, conversational language
  • Smart branching (only relevant questions)
  • Optional chat mode for those who prefer it
01
Receive invitation
The attorney sends a secure, personalized link via email. No account creation required.
02
Complete the guided questionnaire
Clients move through an adaptive, intelligent form. The system only asks relevant questions based on previous answers (marital status, children, residency, etc.). Primary mode: Clean step-by-step wizard  •  Optional mode: Conversational chat
03
Review and submit
Clients can review all answers before submitting. The platform saves progress automatically.
04
Wait for attorney review
After submission, the client is notified that their attorney will prepare the documents and contact them to review.
Important: Clients do not receive documents directly from the system. All communication and document delivery happens through the attorney.

The Core Promise

The attorney stays in full professional control. The client gets a modern, respectful experience. Every document remains unmistakably yours.
